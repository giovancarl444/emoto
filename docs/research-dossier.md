## 2. EU (Reg. 168/2013) + Swedish vehicle classification

Regulation (EU) 168/2013 places all light two-wheelers in category **L**. Within L1e (ceiling 45 km/h / 4 kW continuous), **L1e-A "powered cycle"** is capped at 25 km/h and 1,000 W, while **L1e-B "moped"** runs >25–45 km/h up to 4,000 W. Anything faster than 45 km/h (or >50 cc combustion) is **L3e "motorcycle,"** split by performance into A1 (≤11 kW), A2 (≤35 kW) and A3. Sweden implements L1e-B as **moped klass I** (EU-moped): AM licence from 15, helmet, mandatory trafikförsäkring, register + rear plate — but no vehicle tax and no periodic inspection. **Moped klass II** is a national 25 km/h / ≤1 kW tier needing only a förarbevis, helmet and insurance, with no registration or plate. A pedal-assist e-bike (EPAC) ≤250 W that cuts assist at 25 km/h and only assists while pedalling is legally a **bicycle**. All ten claims in this dimension verified as substantively **confirmed**.

  Sources: [Transportstyrelsen elcykel](https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/sv/vagtrafik/fordon/fordonsregler/regler-for-olika-fordonsslag/moped/Elcykel/)
- **Motorcycle licence ages.** A1 (≤125 cc, ≤11 kW, ≤0.1 kW/kg) from 16; A2 (≤35 kW, ≤0.2 kW/kg) from 18; A (unlimited) from 24, or 20 with two years' A2. *(confidence: high)*
  Sources: [Transportstyrelsen MC](https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/sv/vagtrafik/korkort/ta-korkort/valj-behorighet/motorcykel/), [Transportstyrelsen A2](https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/sv/vagtrafik/korkort/ta-korkort/valj-behorighet/motorcykel/a2-mellanstor-motorcykel/)
- **L3e registration/tax/inspection.** L3e must be registered + rear plate, trafikförsäkring, helmet; unlike mopeds it is subject to vehicle tax (**180 SEK per tax year**) and periodic inspection (a missed besiktning triggers an automatic driving ban). *(confidence: high)*
  Sources: [Transportstyrelsen MC](https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/sv/vagtrafik/fordon/fordonsregler/regler-for-olika-fordonsslag/mc/), [Transportstyrelsen besiktning MC](https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/sv/vagtrafik/fordon/aga-kopa-eller-salja-fordon/fordonsbesiktning/besiktningsregler/motorcykel/)

  Sources: [Transportstyrelsen moped klass I](https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/sv/vagtrafik/fordon/fordonsregler/regler-for-olika-fordonsslag/moped/Moped-klass-I/)

## 3. Registering an imported EU vehicle in Sweden

To road-register an imported EU-type-approved electric two-wheeler, the vehicle must be entered into **vägtrafikregistret** (Transportstyrelsen). The pivotal document is the **Certificate of Conformity (CoC / "intyg om överensstämmelse")**; without it the vehicle generally cannot be registered or insured. Two routes exist: **Route A** — the retailer registers as a **"registrerad yrkesmässig importör"** and may then register *new* (never-taken-into-use) vehicles directly on the CoC, skipping ursprungskontroll; **Route B** (standard) — **ursprungskontroll** (origin check, ~1,240 kr, valid 5 years) → **registreringsbesiktning** at an accredited inspector → register entry → registreringsbevis + plates. For a new EU-type-approved vehicle the besiktning is an identity/document check, not a full technical exam. The verification pass's headline correction: the standard **plate fee is now ~80 kr per plate (not ~62)**, a first-in-11-years increase.

- **CoC.** Proves EU/EC type-approval (EG-typgodkännande), issued by the manufacturer, required to register *and* insure; for EG-typgodkända vehicles no further technical documentation is required; a moped without a CoC generally cannot be insured or registered. *(confidence: high)*
  Sources: [Transportstyrelsen CoC-MC](https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/sv/vagtrafik/fordon/aga-kopa-eller-salja-fordon/import-och-export-av-fordon/fordonsimport-och-ursprungskontroll/import-fran-ett-eu-land/coc-mc/)
- **Registered professional importer route (Route A).** A retailer can register as a "registrerad yrkesmässig importör" and register *new* vehicles directly on the CoC without ursprungskontroll; used vehicles always need ursprungskontroll. Evidence of professional activity centres on a Bolagsverket registration certificate (<12 months) + a Skatteverket extract, with a leverantörsavtal/generalagent agreement or a list of recently imported vehicles as supporting proof — confirm exact attachments against the current application form. *(confidence: medium)* **[VERIFY]**
  Sources: [Transportstyrelsen registrerad importör](https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/sv/vagtrafik/fordon/aga-kopa-eller-salja-fordon/import-och-export-av-fordon/fordonsimport-och-ursprungskontroll/registrerad-importor-yrkesmassig-tillverkare/)

  Sources: [Transportstyrelsen regbevis-EU](https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/sv/vagtrafik/fordon/aga-kopa-eller-salja-fordon/import-och-export-av-fordon/fordonsimport-och-ursprungskontroll/import-fran-ett-eu-land/regbevis-eu/)
- **Ursprungskontroll fee.** ~1,240 kr (2026), paid in advance, non-refundable regardless of outcome (corroborated by two import-guide sources citing Transportstyrelsen; official avgift page 403'd). *(confidence: medium)* **[VERIFY]**
  Sources: [importguiden.se](https://importguiden.se/guider/ursprungskontroll), [tullify.se](https://tullify.se/importera-bil/)

  Sources: [besikta.se](https://besikta.se/tjanster/registreringsbesiktning/sa-funkar-det/), [bilprovningen.se](https://www.bilprovningen.se/tjanster/registreringsbesiktning/oregistrerat-fordon)

## 4. Swedish VAT (moms) & cross-border commerce

Sweden's standard VAT (moms) is **25%** (reduced rates 12% and 6%; motorcycles are standard-rated 25%). Under prisinformationslagen (EU Dir. 98/6/EC), consumer prices must be shown **VAT-inclusive**. Sweden uses the **krona (SEK)**, not the euro. For B2C sales to other EU consumers, a single **EUR 10,000/yr** distance-selling threshold applies (below it, Swedish 25%; above it, destination VAT via Union OSS). On Incoterms, **DDP** puts import duty + import VAT + clearance on the seller; **DAP** on the buyer. For a China-built vehicle imported into the EU, MFN customs duty on electric motorcycles (CN 8711 60) is **~6%**, plus 25% Swedish import VAT on (CIF + duty + freight). Critically, the EU's **7.8%–35.3% countervailing duties on Chinese BEVs apply only to passenger cars (8703 80), NOT motorcycles (8711)** — and the anti-dumping duty on Chinese e-bikes applies to pedelecs, not full e-motorcycles. ## 5. Swedish/EU consumer law

A premium e-motorcycle sold online in Sweden is a **distansavtal** under Lag (2005:59) (implementing the EU Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU), so the buyer has a **14-day ångerrätt** (no reason required) from taking physical possession. If the customer inspects/test-rides at the premises before buying, it is **not** a distance contract and there is no statutory ångerrätt. Two nuances are decisive for a vehicle retailer: the **"specialtillverkad" (made-to-order) exception is narrow** and does *not* cover factory options chosen from the standard range; and **registering/riding does not extinguish the withdrawal right**, though the trader may deduct for värdeminskning from excessive handling. Separately, konsumentköplagen (2022:260) gives a **3-year reklamationsrätt** with a strengthened 2-year presumption of an at-delivery defect. Load-bearing for a 2026 launch: **from 19 June 2026 an "ångerknapp" (withdrawal button) is mandatory** in the same online interface where the contract was concluded. All ten claims verified as **confirmed**.

**Key facts:**

- **14-day ångerrätt (online).** No reason required, from physical possession. Two conditions: the seller must be a näringsidkare, and the sale must run through an organised distance-selling system (basis 2 kap 10 § / period from possession 2 kap 12 §). *(confidence: high)*
  Sources: [Konsumentverket ångerrätt bilköp](https://www.konsumentverket.se/varor-och-tjanster-process/angerratt-bilkop/), [Lag 2005:59](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-200559-om-distansavtal-och-avtal-utanfor_sfs-2005-59/)
- **Showroom purchase = no ångerrätt.** A contract concluded on the premises after in-person inspection/test-drive is binding. Caveat: a hybrid "test-ride then sign online" model is fact-specific (turns on where/how the contract is concluded and whether an organised distance-selling system is used) — treat the exemption as directional, not guaranteed. *(confidence: high)*
  Sources: [Konsumentverket ångerrätt bilköp](https://www.konsumentverket.se/varor-och-tjanster-process/angerratt-bilkop/)
- **"Made-to-order" exception is narrow.** Does NOT cover "a car with extra equipment chosen from the manufacturer's range" (ARN referat 2022-03728; prop. 2019/20:63) — genuine individualisation (bespoke measurements, engraving, special component order) is required; such a vehicle keeps full ångerrätt. *(confidence: high — strongly confirmed)* **[VERIFY]** (§ numbering)
  Sources: [ARN referat 2022-03728](https://www.arn.se/globalassets/extern/pdfer/referat-2022/arendereferat-2022-03728.pdf), [prop. 2019/20:63](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/proposition/tydligare-regler-vid-konsumentavtal_h70363/html/)
- **Use/registration → value-diminution deduction.** Withdrawal survives registration/use; under 2 kap 15 § the consumer compensates for value reduction from handling beyond what was needed to establish the vehicle's characteristics/function. ARN's ~12 kr/mil benchmark is a **nyttoavdrag in hävning (defect-rescission) car cases — NOT the statutory withdrawal deduction** — so treat it as an analogy/upper-orientation; the nyttoavdrag can never exceed the actual värdeminskning. *(confidence: medium)* **[VERIFY]**
  Sources: [d-jur.se nyttoavdrag](https://d-jur.se/kopratt/nyttoavdrag/), [Lag 2005:59](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-200559-om-distansavtal-och-avtal-utanfor_sfs-2005-59/)
- **Return-transport cost.** The consumer pays return shipping only if pre-informed (2 kap 13 § / CRD Art 14); the trader must still refund the outbound cost, capped at its cheapest standard option. If not pre-informed, the trader cannot pass return costs on — so the storefront terms must state the return cost/estimate (load-bearing for a heavy vehicle). *(confidence: high)*
  Sources: [Konsumentverket frakt-återbetalning](https://www.konsumentverket.se/fragor-och-svar/2942147/far-jag-tillbaka-pengarna-for-frakten-nar-jag-angrar-mig/)
- **Refund timeframe.** Within 14 days of the withdrawal notice (2 kap 14 §), same payment method, at no cost to the consumer; for goods the trader may withhold until it receives the goods back or the consumer proves dispatch. The "30 days" figure some databases surface reflects the pre-2014-CRD statute. *(confidence: high)*
  Sources: [Konsumentverket informationskrav](https://www.konsumentverket.se/marknadsratt-foretag/informationskrav-vid-distansavtal-regler-for-foretag/)
- **Pre-contract information + ångerblankett.** 2 kap 2 § (CRD Art 6) requires main characteristics, trader identity, total price incl. taxes and delivery/return costs, payment/delivery terms, reklamation rules, and the withdrawal right's conditions/time-limit/procedure plus the standard ångerblankett. *(confidence: high)*
  Sources: [Konsumentverket informationskrav](https://www.konsumentverket.se/marknadsratt-foretag/informationskrav-vid-distansavtal-regler-for-foretag/)
- **Failure to inform → +12-month extension.** If the trader does not correctly inform about ångerrätt, the window extends up to 12 months beyond the normal 14 days (2 kap 12 § / CRD Art 10); during an uninformed extension the trader also bears the value-reduction risk. *(confidence: high)*
  Sources: [Konsumentverket ångerrätt](https://www.konsumentverket.se/konsumentratt-process/angerratt/)
- **19 June 2026 mandatory ångerknapp.** Online traders must provide a clearly labelled, easily accessible withdrawal function (e.g. "ångra avtalet här") in the same interface where the contract was concluded, available for the full withdrawal period. It creates no new withdrawal right — only easier exercise. Non-compliance can be otillbörlig marknadsföring. **The storefront must ship with a compliant button.** *(confidence: high)* **[VERIFY]**
  Sources: [regeringen.se](https://www.regeringen.se/pressmeddelanden/2025/11/konsumentskyddet-starks-for-avtal-online/), [cms.law](https://cms.law/sv/swe/legal-updates/ny-aangerknapp-kraevs-paa-e-handelsplattformar-fraan-den-19-juni-2026)
- **3-year reklamationsrätt.** Konsumentköplag (2022:260, in force 1 May 2022): the trader is liable for defects present at delivery that appear within 3 years — a statutory right, not a garanti; a reklamation within 2 months of discovery is always timely. *(confidence: high)*
  Sources: [Konsumentköplag 2022:260](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/konsumentkoplag-2022260_sfs-2022-260/), [Konsumentverket reklamera vara](https://www.konsumentverket.se/konsumentratt-process/reklamera-vara/)
- **2-year reversed burden of proof.** Under konsumentköplagen 4 kap 7 § (implementing Dir. 2019/771), for the first 2 years the trader must prove the defect did NOT exist at delivery (up from 6 months under the old Act) — a two-tier structure of 2-year presumption + 3-year total window. *(confidence: high)*
  Sources: [Konsumentköplag 2022:260](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/konsumentkoplag-2022260_sfs-2022-260/)
- **Disputes / upcoming reform.** ARN (Allmänna reklamationsnämnden) and Hallå Konsument handle guidance/disputes; SOU 2025:34 "Ett modernare konsumentskydd vid distansavtal" signals further reform beyond the June 2026 button — worth monitoring. *(confidence: medium)* **[VERIFY]**
  Sources: [SOU 2025:34](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/statens-offentliga-utredningar/ett-modernare-konsumentskydd-vid-distansavtal_hdb334/html/)

## 7. Market sizing

## 8. Warranty norms & reliability pain points

Sur-Ron's baseline manufacturer warranty is **short and dealer-dependent — 12 months** on chassis/swing-arm/motor/controller and 12 months on the battery (battery capped at 20,000 miles / ~32,000 km), with several official UK dealers advertising **18 months**, so the real-world norm is **12–18 months**. Wear items are excluded (or ~6 months); competition/closed-circuit and commercial/rental use is limited to ~30 days; tuning/non-official parts void coverage; and warranty is non-transferable. **Stark Future is the premium benchmark** (2 years standard, 3 on the MX 1.0, optional +2 to reach 4 outside North America). Critically for a Swedish retailer, **konsumentköplagen's 3-year reklamationsrätt overrides any shorter manufacturer term** — the *seller*, not Sur-Ron, carries de-facto multi-year liability. On reliability, the motor is regarded as robust, but the dominant owner complaints — **locked stock controller, BMS/connector faults and melting battery connectors, weak KKE/DNM forks, undersized MTB-style brakes, chain/sprocket wear, disliked tyres, water-ingress faults** — map almost exactly onto a premium upgrade-parts catalog.
