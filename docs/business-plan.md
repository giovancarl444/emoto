## 1. Executive summary

The Nordic market for high-performance light electric two-wheelers (Sur-Ron and its class) is growing structurally, but it is served almost entirely by **template price-shops**: WordPress/WooCommerce brochures, English text on Swedish domains, "was/now" discount theatre, and the single biggest purchase blocker — *road-legality and registration* — buried or ignored. There is **no premium, Swedish-native retailer brand** that owns trust, compliance clarity and experience. The one credible premium light-EV brand in Sweden, CAKE, went bankrupt in February 2024 — proving the appetite exists and the reseller layer is wide open.

**ELDR** ("eld" = Swedish for *fire*; "el-" reads *electric*) is that brand: an independent, flagship-grade retailer selling Sur-Ron today, architected to carry a second and third marque tomorrow. We win on four wedges the incumbents leave open:

1. **Compliance concierge** — a productized registration service (CoC → ursprungskontroll → registreringsbesiktning → plates) that turns the #1 anxiety into a checkout add-on.
2. **Trust & clarity** — fixed honest pricing, real Swedish copy, plain-language L1e vs L3e explainers, transparent warranty layered on the statutory 3-year reklamationsrätt.
3. **Configurator-grade experience** — a fast, mobile-first storefront with off-road ↔ street-legal toggles that show top speed, licence and registration consequences inline.
4. **Attach-rate upgrades** — a curated parts catalogue built around the *known* Sur-Ron failure points (controllers, forks, brakes, connectors, tyres).

**Base-case trajectory (modelled):** 162 vehicles and **~9.7 MSEK net revenue in Year 1**, scaling to 378 vehicles and **~22.5 MSEK in Year 2**, ~21 % blended gross margin, **EBITDA break-even in month 15**, with a peak operating-cash trough of ~0.7 MSEK. Total launch capital required (incl. inventory buffer and contingency): **~1.5–2.5 MSEK** [VERIFY against final supplier terms].

## 3. Competitive analysis

The category is **functional, fragmented and price-led** — which is precisely the opening.

| Competitor | Who / URL | Price band (SEK) | Strength | Weakness we exploit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ridesurron.se** (primary target) | KOHLA AB, org 556608-3787, Saltsjö-Boo | ~58,500–119,900 | Full range, "Sur-Ron Sweden" positioning | Fragmented two-site build (WP brochure + separate shop), English-first on a .se domain, thin trust signals |
| surronsweden.com | Surron Sverige | ~52,000–84,000 | Strong reassurance copy, fast dispatch | "Was/now" discount theatre that erodes trust |
| Levante Örebro | levanteorebro.com | ~39,990–84,990 | Deep parts, test rides, plates for L3e | Generic presentation; local, not national brand |
| EV Specialisten | ev-specialisten.se | financing from ~1,839 kr/mo | Multi-brand + financing framing | Pricing buried; generic collection pages |
| All4Ride | all4ride.co (FR) | pan-EU | Content/SEO moat, 2-yr motor warranty, assembled delivery | **No Swedish physical presence or local trust** |

**Structural notes:** Sur-Ron's official Swedish general agent is **Adoy AB**, which underpins warranty for independents — a channel we formalise. Premium context: **CAKE** (Stockholm, premium e-moto) filed for bankruptcy Feb 2024 and was acquired by Norway's Brages Holding — the premium *brand* space is proven but the premium *retail* layer is empty.

**How we win (the whitespace, verbatim from research):** (1) clean fixed transparent pricing; (2) one cohesive site; (3) native, well-written Swedish; (4) prominent third-party reviews + visible company legitimacy; (5) a compliance concierge demystifying L1e (45 km/h moped) vs L3e (≈90 km/h light-MC) registration and plates; (6) cold-climate/winter battery guidance; (7) showroom/test-ride + service network; (8) curated gear/upgrade bundling.

## 5. Product & pricing strategy

### 5.1 Catalogue (three revenue layers)
- **Vehicles (core revenue):** Sur-Ron **Light Bee X** (lightweight enduro; off-road + road-legal **L1e** moped), **Ultra Bee** (mid-size; off-road + road-legal **L3e** — note: both the R and T trims are L3e ~90 km/h, they differ in tyre/rim spec, *not* road class), **Storm Bee** (full-size flagship; enduro E + street L3e F). Specs are editable content, marked [VERIFY] where a manufacturer "peak" figure must be confirmed against the vehicle CoC.
- **Parts & accessories (margin + attach):** curated around real owner pain points — sport controllers, KKE forks, 250 mm brake kits, sealed battery connectors, performance rims/tyres, smoother throttles, CNC footpegs, bar-mounted risers.
- **Services (differentiator + recurring):** registration help, delivery, warranty handling.

### 5.2 Pricing (incl. 25 % moms) and margin logic
Indicative list prices, positioned at the upper-mid of the observed SEK bands — held firm and bundled with trust rather than discounted:

| Model / variant | List (incl. VAT) | Class |
|---|---|---|
| Light Bee X — off-road | 54,900 | off-road |
| Light Bee X — L1e road | 59,900 | L1e-B moped |
| Ultra Bee — off-road | 64,900 | off-road |
| Ultra Bee — R / T (L3e road) | 79,900 / 84,900 | L3e |
| Storm Bee — E off-road | 99,900 | off-road |
| Storm Bee — F street | 119,900 | L3e |
| Registration help | 3,495 (+ official fees) | service |

**Margin logic (net of VAT):** blended vehicle gross margin **~20 %** (thin, as with all resellers, but defended by bundling and no discount theatre); parts **~45 %**; registration service **~55 %** (labour-led). The strategy is deliberate: use vehicles as the trust-building anchor at honest margin, and drive contribution through **attach** (parts + registration + gear) where margins are richer. A key margin tailwind confirmed in research: **the EU's 7.8–35.3 % countervailing duties on Chinese EVs apply only to passenger cars (HS 8703), NOT motorcycles (HS 8711)** — our imports face only the ~6 % MFN duty [VERIFY], protecting landed cost.

### 5.3 Financing
Klarna and partner instalment financing (framed as "from X kr/mo") to de-risk the five-figure decision — matching EV Specialisten's proven lever, executed with premium clarity.

## 6. Go-to-market

**Launch sequence:**
1. **Pre-launch (M-2→0):** brand + waitlist, SEO foundation, 10–15 cornerstone articles targeting `elcrossmoped`, `Sur-Ron köpa`, `vägregistrerad L3e`, `L1e moped`, model terms; seed inventory of in-stock hero units.
2. **Soft launch (M1–3):** Sweden only, sv+en; performance marketing on high-intent search + Meta/YouTube retargeting; founder-led content and real reviews.
3. **Scale (M4–12):** widen catalogue, formalise Adoy AB warranty channel, add showroom/test-ride, referral + email/SMS lifecycle.
4. **Nordics (M13–24):** NO/FI/DK via OSS VAT + localized pages and hreflang already built into the storefront.

**Channels:** high-intent SEO/SEM (the compliance content is a durable moat, mirroring All4Ride's approach but Swedish-native), Meta/TikTok/YouTube for terrain/performance content, email/SMS (Klaviyo) lifecycle, reviews/UGC (Judge.me/Trustpilot), referral, and partnerships with riding clubs/tracks.

## 7. Operations & fulfillment

- **Sourcing:** authorized supply via the Sur-Ron channel / general agent Adoy AB and vetted EU distributors; ELDR pursues **registered professional importer** status with Transportstyrelsen to register new vehicles directly on the CoC and skip ursprungskontroll — a real operational moat and a faster customer promise.
- **Inventory & lead-time logic:** a small **in-stock buffer** of hero units (ships in 2–5 days from a Swedish warehouse) plus **build-to-order** for the long tail (45–120 days, factory build + ship) — the storefront tells this truth per variant. Build-to-order minimises working-capital risk on a high-ticket catalogue.
- **Shipping:** **DDP within the EU** (VAT + duty paid, no border surprises), **DAP outside**; delivered assembled and ride-ready.
- **Returns & warranty:** 14-day distance-selling withdrawal handled honestly (value-diminution deduction for use beyond inspection), ELDR warranty on vehicle + battery layered above the statutory 3-year reklamationsrätt (2-year reversed burden of proof).
- **Registration service:** the productized concierge — CoC handling, ursprungskontroll, booking registreringsbesiktning, plates, and insurance guidance.
- **Order status & support:** account + order status via Shopify; real Swedish-language support as a brand pillar.

## 9. Risks & mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **Regulatory** (L1e/L3e rules, registration process, youth-EV scrutiny) | Compliance built into product/CMS; [VERIFY] discipline; registered-importer status; monitor SOU 2025:34 and the 19 Jun 2026 ångerknapp rule |
| **Supply** (single-marque dependency, factory lead times) | Formalise Adoy AB channel; multi-brand architecture ready for a 2nd/3rd marque; in-stock buffer + honest ETAs |
| **Warranty / liability** (short 12-mo manufacturer term vs 3-yr statutory) | Price the statutory 3-year liability into margin; ELDR-backed 24-mo terms; exclusions honest and explicit |
| **FX / landed cost** (SEK vs supplier currency, ~6 % duty) | Price buffers, forward-buying hero SKUs; motorcycles exempt from China-EV countervailing duties (margin protection) |
| **Brand/legal** (reseller vs marque) | Independent, ownable brand; "Sur-Ron® is a trademark of its owner" disclaimer; no counterfeiting/misrepresentation |
| **Demand** (niche el-MC, seasonality) | Youth AM + off-road recreational base; parts/attach revenue smooths seasonality; Nordic expansion |
